# Draft coverage schedule

**Status: draft only; not operative.** This schedule describes proposed terms for review. It must not be used as a live policy, offer, or service commitment until legal, regulatory, underwriting, and operational review is complete.

## Proposed monthly coverage price and choice

Draft price assumption: **$5 per month**. At enrollment, the customer selects exactly one option; the provider must record that selection. Do not preselect, combine, or silently change it.

- [ ] **Monthly cadence:** up to one assisted rebuild in each billing month.
- [ ] **12-month cadence:** up to one assisted rebuild in each consecutive 12-month coverage period.

Both choices are shown at the same draft monthly price. The 12-month option is not an annual payment; it is one assisted rebuild opportunity per 12 months while coverage remains active. Clarify the billing date, what counts as one rebuild, claim-window, concurrency, rollover, cancellation, waiting period, and whether unused assistance expires before any launch.

## Separate template assumption

The proposed **$49 one-time template** is separate from monthly coverage and provides self-hosted files/setup guidance only. It does not itself include assisted rebuild work. Refund treatment for a delivered template remains subject to the separate draft refund terms and applicable law.

## Scope

If approved, assistance would be limited to the documented six layers in `../offer/six-layers.md`, the selected cadence, written claim acceptance, and available customer-owned artifacts. Credentials are never collected; the customer performs reconnection on their host. Provider downtime, unavailable accounts, unsupported software, and missing source data may prevent completion.

## Open decisions before use

Set the exact claim eligibility and waiting period, covered operating systems/providers, service hours, supported workload, time zone, start/stop of any service clock, claim prioritization, labor limits, privacy retention, refunds, and taxes. Do not infer these terms from the draft prices.
