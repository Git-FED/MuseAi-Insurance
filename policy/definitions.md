# Draft definitions

These working definitions are for review and do not create a binding policy.

- **Six layers:** identity + memory; workspace; handoff; credentials (secure reconnect only); connections; schedules, as described in `../offer/six-layers.md`.
- **Selected cadence:** the one mutually exclusive option recorded at enrollment: one assisted rebuild per billing month, or one assisted rebuild every 12 months while coverage is active.
- **Assisted rebuild:** documented help to restore supported customer-owned artifacts and guide the customer through the approved acceptance checklist. It does not mean copying a secret to the provider or guaranteeing identical behavior.
- **Complete claim:** a claim accepted in writing after the customer supplies required non-secret evidence and identifies affected layer(s), environment, and requested help.
- **Rebuild complete:** every pre-agreed must-pass acceptance check for the incident is recorded as passed in the proof report. A blocked or skipped check is not a pass.
- **Credential:** password, API token/key, private key, recovery code, cookie, MFA value, or other authentication secret. The customer, not this template/provider, enters credentials into their account or trusted local secret store.
- **Coverage fee:** monthly fee for the selected assistance cadence, if legally approved. It is distinct from the proposed one-time template purchase.
- **Provider / customer:** placeholders whose legal identities and responsibilities must be completed before use.
