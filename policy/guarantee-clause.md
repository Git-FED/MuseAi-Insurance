# Draft service target and rebuild-completion clause

**Draft only; not an operative guarantee.** The language below is a proposed definition for legal and operational review. Do not advertise or accept payment against it until the provider has confirmed feasibility, staffing, exclusions, and compliant product structure.

## Proposed clock

If approved, a proposed 48-hour target would begin only after the provider confirms in writing that a complete claim is accepted, the customer has supplied the required non-secret artifacts, and the customer is available for required decisions. Define the time zone, service hours, pauses, provider-caused delays, customer-caused delays, and interaction with holidays before use. A submitted form alone does not start the clock.

## Proposed completion test

Before coverage begins, the customer and provider would agree on a short, written acceptance checklist for the affected layers. A proposed rebuild-complete state requires:

1. the agreed customer-owned files/configuration are restored to the specified destination;
2. the customer has completed credential re-entry through the provider’s official UI or trusted local secret store (the provider does not receive credential values);
3. each required connection and schedule has a customer-approved verification step; and
4. all must-pass checks on the signed acceptance checklist pass, with results recorded in a dated proof report.

A passing checklist demonstrates only that those checks passed at that time. It does not guarantee identical output, future uptime, provider availability, or freedom from defects. If a provider or customer decision blocks a check, record it as blocked, not passed.

## Proposed failure/refund concept

If the approved must-pass checks cannot be completed within the agreed target for a reason within the provider’s responsibility, a proposed remedy is a refund of the coverage fees paid to date for the affected coverage relationship; the delivered one-time template would be treated separately. The exact amount, eligibility, claim review, and payment timing must be specified in `../claims/refund-trigger.md` and reviewed by counsel. This paragraph is not a promise or authorization to sell.
