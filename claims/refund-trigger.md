# Draft refund trigger

**Draft-only proposal; not an operative refund policy.** Counsel and operations must review these terms, consumer-law treatment, payment method, timing, and regulated-product implications before use.

## Proposed trigger

If a claim is accepted in writing, the customer has completed all stated dependencies, and the provider fails to complete the agreed must-pass rebuild checks within the proposed 48-hour target for a reason within the provider’s responsibility, the proposed remedy is a refund of the coverage fees paid to date for that customer’s coverage relationship.

The proposed one-time template fee is separate because the template files have already been delivered. This draft does not resolve statutory cancellation/refund rights or any situation in which law requires a different result.

## Required record

Keep a claim reference, selected cadence, coverage start date, acceptance timestamp, dependencies and pauses, agreed must-pass checks, dated proof report, stated failure reason, refund calculation, decision-maker, and customer notification. Do not put credentials or full payment-card data in the claim record.

## Decisions still required

Define claim acceptance, clock pauses, provider-responsibility test, partial success, customer-caused delay, disputes/appeals, refund destination and timing, cancellation, taxes, claim limits, and retention. Do not describe this draft as a guarantee until these are finalized and approved.
