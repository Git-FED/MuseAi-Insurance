# Draft offer — six-layer rebuild assistance

**Status: DRAFT / NOT FOR SALE.** Legal, regulatory, tax, underwriting, support, and operational review is required. “Insurance” terminology may be regulated; do not use it until qualified counsel and the relevant regulator/underwriter approve the structure.

## What is contemplated

A self-hosted customer workflow for documenting an incident and, where feasible, assisting a rebuild across six layers. The customer controls infrastructure and credentials. This template does not access, transmit, or store secrets.

## Draft choices at $5/month

The customer must select **one and only one** of the following; the provider must record the selection explicitly:

- [ ] **Monthly option:** one assisted rebuild per month.
- [ ] **Annual option:** one assisted rebuild every 12 months.

Both options are draft assumptions at the same **$5/month** price. No option is preselected. Define rollover, unused service, scope, response, and cancellation terms before launch.

## Other draft assumption

A **$49 one-time template** may provide files and setup guidance only. It is not a guarantee of successful recovery. A proposed **48-hour target** and a **refund if rebuild fails** require written definitions, evidence standards, exclusions, staffing capacity, and legal review.
