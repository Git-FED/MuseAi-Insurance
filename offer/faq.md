# Draft FAQ

**Is this insurance?** Not by this repository. “Insurance” and related claims may be regulated; counsel and regulators must review the structure.

**Which $5 option do I get?** Neither is selected automatically. Choose either one assisted rebuild per month or one assisted rebuild every 12 months; they are mutually exclusive draft options at the same assumed price.

**Do you receive my API keys?** No. Credentials and secrets stay on the customer’s host. Do not paste them into claims, logs, tickets, or this portal.

**Is 48 hours guaranteed?** No. It is a draft target pending capacity, definitions, exclusions, and legal/operational approval.

**Do I automatically get a refund if recovery fails?** Not under this draft. A reviewed refund trigger and process must be adopted first.
