The draft workflow

Five steps. Six layers. Zero magic wands.

The template is designed to help people document and verify what can be rebuilt. It does not recover deleted provider data or promise that an integration will still work.

Package→Import→Reconnect→Schedule→Prove
01

Identity + memory

Keep stable notes and provenance organized.

02

Workspace

Gather the files, prompts, and docs you control.

03

Handoff

Write down what exists and what remains uncertain.

04

Credentials

You reconnect accounts yourself; secrets stay yours.

05

Connections

Re-establish integrations one at a time.

06

Schedules

Review schedules before deciding whether to restore them.

Designed for the “uh-oh” moment

A tiny bot with a tiny roll of tape is not a recovery strategy.

This draft favors a human-reviewed checklist over confident promises. The customer controls their host, files, accounts, and secrets at every step.
